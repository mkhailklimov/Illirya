## Priority 1: next development

### 1. Game concept: "The Governor"
The game is an AI game master. The player is a governor sent from Rome by Emperor Tiberius in AD 30 to develop the harbour town of Acruvium (today's Kotor). More cities can be added later. The tasks:
- surround it with farmland to prevent famine;
- develop crafts for household goods and military equipment;
- set up spinning and weaving workshops for clothing;
- develop winemaking and olive oil production;
- trade with other cities by sea and land, and build roads.

The challenges are tribute demanded by Rome, locals rebelling against high taxes, and an army that keeps order but costs money: a vicious cycle. Droughts, heavy rains, earthquakes, pirate raids, peasant uprisings and raids by neighbouring peoples add pressure. The goal is a sensible balance that turns Illyria into a prosperous, autonomous state.

Raiders follow the period (Thracians, Dacians, Sarmatians, Adriatic pirates). Huns and Bulgars arrive centuries later and are kept for a possible later-era expansion.

Full concept: [docs/governor-concept.md](docs/governor-concept.md) (core loop, city numbers, events, endings, how the AI game master works and a draft system prompt).

### 2. Design direction
- **Keep** the ancient mosaic and fresco style of the current visuals (see [design/style-guide.md](design/style-guide.md)).
- **Change the lettering** to a style reminiscent of an *Abecedarium* (ancient carved and painted Roman letters). **Decided 11 October 2026: the pair.** Caesar Dressing for the logo and big titles, Marcellus for names, dates and small labels. Cinzel is replaced in the style guide and all image sources, and the images are re-rendered.

## Later

### More cities to build and develop
The game starts in Acruvium. As it grows, players will be able to found or take over other cities:
- **Salona** (near Split): capital of Roman Dalmatia, the biggest prize.
- **Epidaurum** (Cavtat): the AD 30 city on the Dubrovnik coast. Chosen over Dubrovnik for historical realism.
- **Dubrovnik (Ragusa):** founded around the 7th century by refugees from Epidaurum, so it belongs to a later era. It can appear when the game jumps forward in time, or as Epidaurum's "future" upgrade.
- Others to consider: Risinium (Risan), Butua (Budva), Narona, Scodra, Dyrrachium.
