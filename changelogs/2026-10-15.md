# 2026-10-15

Playtest notes on the browser game (v4), all nine handled. Published as **version 5** at the same link: https://claude.ai/artifact/SBg4CFaAq91RdLq7AFo6jX

### 1. Six places on the AD 30 map
- The map showed 10 places (Acruvium plus 9 trading towns). The rule now: **6 for Illyria: AD 30, 9 for AD 120, up to 12 for AD 180.**
- AD 30: **Acruvium, Epidaurum, Salona, Aquileia, Dyrrachium, Brundisium.** Salona was chosen as the sixth: the provincial capital, halfway up the coast, and it sells timber, marble and iron (the scarce goods).
- **Narona, Iader, Pola and Ancon** moved to `rules/towns-later.csv`, ready for AD 120 (with Ravenna, Tergeste and Scodra already reserved).
- With Narona and Pola gone nobody bought salt, so **Salona now buys salt** (×1.2). Grain by convoy now comes only from Dyrrachium (or the merchants at the quay).

### 2. Towns drawn as towns
- Every town on the Adriatic map is a small walled town with three towers and a gate, instead of a dot. Acruvium is a larger gold one instead of the star. A selected town turns gold.

### 3. Lettering
- The Times-like font was Cormorant Garamond (speech, card hints, history notes). It is gone: all running text is **Marcellus**, titles stay **Caesar Dressing**. The "watches…" line on the advisor cards is Marcellus too.

### 4. Cards
- The season's cards now sit **at the top of the page**, right under the numbers; the decision panel and the season report appear there too, so nothing is hidden below the map.
- Unturned cards glow and pulse; when a card asks for a decision, that card pulses brighter and the others dim.
- Sounds about three times quieter (page turn, gong, dice).

### 5. Plans: take single orders
- Each line of an advisor's plan has a tick box. Untick what you don't want and press **Add ticked**: take the vineyard, leave the wheat.

### 6. Pictures for orders
- All 16 orders have a small drawing (a ship for laying a keel, a ship with an arrow for a convoy, coins up/down for taxes, gallows, scroll, helmet, altar, laurel wreath, gift chest, owl, amphora, axe…). A build order shows the building's own icon. They appear on the action buttons, in the plans and in the season's order list.

### 7. Advisors easier to tell apart
- Colours kept; silhouettes and signs changed. Each coin in the council now carries a **badge with his sign**: galley (Epicadus, fleet), **vine rod** (Antistius, general), **lituus** (Tertius, haruspex), **coins** (Felix, treasury), **scales** (Nikandros, trade).
- Nikandros now wears a wide-brimmed Greek travelling hat (petasos) instead of the headband; Antistius holds the centurion's knotted vine rod instead of the cavalry pennant. Cards re-rendered (`design/source/advisors.py`, `design/pieces/advisors/`); `web/build.py` writes the signs into `web/data.js`.

### 8. City plan from above
- Redrawn as a bird's-eye plan: the **sea at the bottom** with the **mole** (ships moor inside it), the **shipyard** (three slipways, keels shown on them) and the **fort** on a headland; the **forum** in the middle; **five streets** running out of it to the left, up, right, upper-left and upper-right, through gates in the round town wall.
- The 12 town plots line the streets inside the walls, nearest the forum first; the fields are outside the walls left and right, and the four hillside scrub plots (to clear) are the top row.
- The square in the middle is labelled FORVM (the Roman agora).

### 9. Compass
- New **eight-point wind rose** with sharp gold points reaching past the ring, north in crimson, a tick ring.
- The wind: the point on the side the wind comes from glows, and a **Roman wind-head** blows in from that side (as on mosaics and old maps). Dead calm: no head, all points equal. The corner rose is larger, and clicking it shows the same rose large with the wind's Latin name (it used to show the old mosaic picture).

### Checked
- Headless browser: four seasons played through (cards, decisions, dice, a plan with one order unticked, a keel laid, sealing, reports), both tabs, phone width (no sideways scroll); no script errors.

### Not changed / open

## Evening: second round (version 6, same link)

### Sounds
- **Land cards: a Roman cornu call** (a low rising fifth on the bronze horn). The Romans didn't really use war drums; their signals were brass horns (tuba, cornu, bucina). A drum is a one-line swap if wanted.
- **Sea cards: a ship's bell**, struck twice.
- **Omen cards: the gong**, unchanged (approved).

### Advisors
- Since each coin now carries one big sign outside the portrait, the small props inside the portraits are gone (vine rod, lituus and amulet, trident). The silhouettes stay: sailor's cap, crested helmet, petasos, cropped hair, veil.
- The galley sign was redrawn bold (square sail, hull, oars) so it reads at badge size.

### A picture on every card
- All 29 event cards (26 events + 3 quiet ones) have their own small line picture in the gold style, with a touch of colour: the sun over cracked earth (drought), a dark-sailed ship (pirates), a hull drawn in the sand (Epicadus's galley), helmet and coins (pay), salt pans and sacks, two wax tablets that don't agree, the smoking altar and the eye, smoke over the hills, torrents, a tilting temple and a crack, the crowd, sheaves and cart, the hoard, grapes and amphorae, the oil ship, the sealed letter and the standard, the letter between laurels, the screw press, eagles over the forum, lightning on the temple, the storm, the comet, the prophetess between market stalls, the snake on the temple steps, the moon over the sleeper, the birds before the storm, and quiet fields, a still altar, fishing boats.
- The picture band is tinted by deck (land ochre, sea blue, omen purple).

### Fixed
- When a card asked for a decision, the other cards were dimmed in a way that broke the 3D flip, so they showed their backs mirrored. The dimming is now on the card face only.
