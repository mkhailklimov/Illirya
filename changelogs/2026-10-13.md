# 13 October 2026

### Done
- **The game in the browser:** https://claude.ai/artifact/SBg4CFaAq91RdLq7AFo6jX (private until you share it from the page's Share menu).
  - [web/engine.js](../web/engine.js) is a port of `rules/play.py`, formula for formula: weather, the three cards, dice, the council's moods and abilities, A bold / B prudent / C your own words (the offline word matcher from `scribe.py`) / D decrees from the list, convoys, buildings, tribute, lose checks.
  - [web/build.py](../web/build.py) turns `rules/*.csv` and `events/*.yaml` into `web/data.js` and makes the web images. After you change a number or a card: `python web/build.py`, then Claude republishes the same link.
  - The game saves in your browser; "New game" starts again.
  - Checked by a script that played 5 seasons headless without errors (cards, dice, abilities, all three kinds of orders, tribute), plus a phone-width view.
- **Portraits now differ by what each man carries:** Marcus Antistius holds his cohort's crimson pennant (vexillum); Tertius Volusius raises the augur's lituus and wears a bulla, the round amulet against evil; Epicadus has Neptune's trident behind his shoulder.
- **Card lettering** follows the pair: Caesar Dressing for the name and the ability title, Marcellus for the role, labels and the ability text, Cormorant Italic only for "watches…".
- **Six qualities per advisor** (DRAFT, 1–5): experience, decisions, strategy, administration, persuasion, logic, in `rules/advisors.csv`, on the cards and in [formulas.md](../rules/formulas.md). They do not change any roll yet; formulas.md lists possible uses.
- **The d20 in three stones:** obsidian, amber and malachite, with Caesar Dressing numerals (gold inlay; dark resin on amber). 3 × 20 PNGs in `design/pieces/dice/<stone>/`. The flat gold die is gone. In the web game you can switch stone at the bottom of the page.

### Seen in the test games
