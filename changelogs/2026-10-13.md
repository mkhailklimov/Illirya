### Later the same day: rules v2 and the board-game layout
After the first playtest you asked for more actions, longer building times, specific goods, a city map and a sea map, and cards turned one by one. Decided: up to 12 orders a season with no other cap; local goods first, more through trade; the web game is the main version, laid out like a board game (a shared Adriatic map, one city sheet per player; Acruvium is the first city).

- **Two tabs:** *Acruvium* (the city sheet: walls, fortress, quay, ships in harbour and keels on the slipway, 12 town plots and 12 field plots; click a free plot to build, a built one to see its state) and *Adriatic* (the real coastline from Natural Earth, six trading towns with sailing days, your ships at sea; click a town for its prices).
- **The order book:** up to 12 orders, from the seven plans (one from each advisor, plus the scribe's bold and prudent), single actions, or your own words. It checks costs and materials as you go and marks what cannot be done.
- **Cards** lie face down until you click them: a parchment rustle for land and sea, a gong for omens (sound can be switched off at the bottom).
- **Compass and die** sit in the bottom corners all the time; the die rolls in the corner for every voyage home.
- New rule tables: `rules/goods.csv`, `rules/towns.csv`; `buildings.csv` and `decrees.csv` rewritten; ten cards moved from 'goods' to specific goods. Details in [formulas.md](../rules/formulas.md), "Rules v2".
- `rules/play.py` is frozen at v1 and says so when started.
- Checked: 6 seasons clicked through in a headless browser without errors (cards, decisions, dice, plans, plots, a hand-made convoy, both tabs, phone width), and 10 bot games of 40 seasons in the engine.
