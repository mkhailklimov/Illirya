# 2026-10-30

## Version 22 (rules v15): your advisor pictures, sixty years, a council that grows old, useful cards, a hall of fame

### Your five vase figures are in the game
The five red-figure pictures you made are now the advisors in every big pop-up:
- the merchant with his balance and amphora (Nikandros);
- the scribe with his tablet and chest (Felix);
- the officer with his round shield (Antistius);
- the haruspex with the liver and the curled staff (Tertius);
- the captain with his steering oar and rope (Epicadus).

They are kept in `design/pieces/figures/`. The build no longer crops them to fit the tall frame: it adds black above and below, so the balance and the shield stay whole.

### Sixty years, and the governor dies at ninety
The game now runs from AD 30 to the winter of AD 89, 240 seasons. You start at 31, and your age is shown in the header. If no victory comes first, the game ends with your death in your ninetieth year, "and the town buries you by the Sea Gate".

Rome's letters now go on to the end, each on its true date:
- the death of Claudius (54);
- the great fire of Rome (64);
- the death of Nero (68);
- the legions of the Danube marching for Vespasian (69);
- Vespasian's death (79);
- Vesuvius (79);
- Titus's death (81);
- the Dacians crossing the Danube (85).

Each Emperor's death is another chance for the soldiers to proclaim you, and the haruspex can foretell every one of them a year ahead. All the older cards now run to AD 89, apart from two that belong to Tiberius's reign.

### The council grows old
Every advisor has an age, shown next to his name, and the six skills from his card (experience, decisions, strategy, administration, persuasion, logic), each up to five stars.
- **Every year** they get older, and most learn something: one skill rises by a star, often the one that counts on his advice.
- **They can die.** The older the man, the likelier: about 1 in 100 a year under 50, 1 in 4 after 80. A full-size pop-up announces the death with the advisor's figure.
- **A younger, less experienced man takes his place.** He has one star of experience and lower skills, which grow with the years. The successors have names of the time and place: Bato of Rhizon, Lucius Vettius Rufus, Demetrios of Issa, Eutychus, Aulus Spurinna, and others.
- **The new man's name goes everywhere.** On the cards, in the advice and in the letters, the successor's name replaces the dead man's. The dead keep their own names in the record.
- **Click an advisor** for his live card: his figure, age, years in office, his six skills as stars, and his ability.

In a sixty-year game about nine advisors die.

### More varied cards, and useful ones
Thirteen new cards, each with an effect that lasts or that you can act on:
- **Land:**
  - *A plough on wheels*: wheat harvests +25%.
  - *A better loom*: cloth +50%.
  - *Clay by the river*: a brick kiln; every building needs a quarter less timber, and bricks and tiles bring 60 denarii a season.
  - *Plague in the harbour quarter*: a real disaster. Close the quarter, or pray and lose more.
  - *Fever from the marsh*: drain it for a new field plot.
- **Sea:**
  - *A pilot's book of the coast*: +2 on the die whenever a convoy comes home.
  - *A foresail from Alexandria*: convoys trade 8% better.
  - *Thirsty soldiers in Aquileia*: for a year, wine sells 40% dearer there.
- **The gods** (the haruspex, now useful):
  - *The physician who restores sight*: he couches cataracts, as Celsus describes around AD 30. Keep him and plague and fever kill half as many.
  - *The liver shows a lean year*: next summer's harvest will be half. Buy grain now, sow barley too, or wait and go hungry.
  - *The liver shows a rich year*: sell the old grain now, before the new comes in.
  - *Sell your oil in Aquileia*: for a year oil sells 40% dearer there; you will see the price when you send a convoy.
  - *A sickness is coming by sea*: the plague will come within the year. Turn foreign ships away and few will die.

His prophecies come true: the lean year really is lean, and the plague really comes.

### The hall of fame
A new tab, **Hall of fame**, lists every governorship that has ended, best score first: the governor's name (you can type one on the first screen), the player, the score, the years and how it ended ("Died in old age, aged 90", "Victory: …", "Replaced for famine"…).

On the published page the hall is **shared by everyone who plays**: each player's best 25 games are kept with the artifact, and everyone sees all of them.
- **Who can add a result:** to add their results, others need at least "Contributor" access. Viewers see the hall, but their own results stay in their browser only.
- **Local copy:** opened as a file, the hall shows the games played in that browser.
