# 2026-11-15

## Version 31 published (rules v19), with A bumper harvest

Version 31 (navy and purple back, battle numbers per throw, four real outcomes on every decision) was made on 13 November but not tested. Today it was tested and published, together with a new card.

- **New card picture:** A bumper harvest. That makes 10 of the 57 cards.
- **Tested in the browser:**
  - a season of cards on desktop and on a phone: the four outcomes fit under every proposal on a phone, with no sideways scrolling;
  - a tribe's attack: the battle shows the dead and the men left after each throw, and after our victory and mercy the **"ask to join" pop-up** came up on screen and worked (the Labeatae joined);
  - no script errors.
- **Bot simulation, 6 games each:** both versions run with no errors and the random bot finishes no game in either. Version 31 had 3 rebellions against 4, 5 famines against 3, and won 5 tribes against 2. With so few games these differences may be chance.
- **Rules notes:** the table of four outcomes is now in `rules/formulas.md`.
- **Still open:** on a phone the compass and the die in the bottom corners cover the edge of the last proposal.
