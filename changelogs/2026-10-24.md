Your six new thoughts, built. Published as **version 15** at the same link: https://claude.ai/artifact/SBg4CFaAq91RdLq7AFo6jX (rules v11). Saves carry on.

### 1. The season's report as a dashboard
The report now opens with **cards**, one for each number: grain, treasury, people, loyalty, Roman favour, army and ships. Each card shows the value, the change with an arrow (▲ +3 / ▼ −20 / no change) and a small line of the last seasons. A card turns red when the number is dangerous. Below them is a smaller row of cards for the stores: wine, olive oil, fleece, cloth, timber and so on. Hover over a card to see its past values season by season. The old table of changes is gone; the achievements, troubles and the scribe's record follow below.

### 2. More ships, and warships
- **Six berths** in the harbour, for merchant galleys and warships together (a keel on the slipway takes one too).
- **New order: "Lay the keel of a trireme"** (Sea and trade): 900 denarii, 30 timber, 8 iron, 6 cloth, launched in three seasons, 40 denarii a season for rowers and marines. Triremes do not trade; they fight. With one or two in the bay:
  - pirates come less often;
  - a part of their force is sunk at sea before it lands (15% for each trireme);
  - +1 in the battle against them for each, and their ships are easier to take;
  - a convoy attacked by pirates is saved.
- Triremes have their own names (Mars, Bellona, Fulmen...). On the city map they lie in the bay with a dark hull and a ram. The fleet panel lists them, and Epicadus proposes one when the chest allows.

### 3. You can see what the inventions bring
- After the report, **a pop-up** opens in every harvest season: "The screw press brings us 9 more amphorae of olive oil this season: 39 instead of 30, worth about 630 denarii at market", plus the 50 denarii from the neighbours. In other seasons a notice shows the 50 denarii.
- The press no longer waits for a lucky card: **"Commission a screw press"** is a new order (Building and planting; 800 denarii, 4 iron, 6 timber). Nikandros proposes it once you have olives.

### 4. The advisors at full length
The round coins stay on the council. For the important moments a pop-up shows the advisor **standing at full length**, a dark silhouette in his own colour, holding his things:
- **Tertius:** veiled, with the lituus and the liver on a dish, and a small altar smoking.
- **Antistius:** a helmet with a red crest, a cloak, a shield, and the vine rod of command.
- **Epicadus:** a felt cap, a beard, a trident and a coil of rope.
- **Nikandros:** a wide-brimmed hat, a cloak pinned at the shoulder, the scales, and an amphora.
- **Felix:** open wax tablets and a stylus, a purse, and the strongbox at his feet.

They appear when the enemy comes (the commander brings the news), at the judgement of captives, when replacing the fallen, at a rebellion, when an advisor offers his special ability, with the haruspex's reading, and with what the machines brought.

### 5. The priest sees more
A sacrifice at the temple now also shows:
- **Next season's harvest:** the wind is fixed now, and Tertius tells you what the fields will give (grain, wine, oil, fleece...) and how many months the granaries will hold afterwards.
- **Next season's omen card, already drawn:** that card is the one that comes, so a disaster no longer arrives unannounced.

This comes on top of the coming war (+1 forewarned), the blessing on ships at sea, the town's mood and his advice. It all opens as his own pop-up after the report.

### 6. The citadel
It was possible but hard to find, and expensive: 2,500 denarii, 30 timber and 10 iron, which is a lot of iron. To build it, in the orders phase click the **dashed "citadel site" on the crag** (top right of the city map). It now costs 2,500 denarii, **20 timber and 6 iron**, and takes **8 seasons** (was 12). The build list says "on the crag", and if something is missing it says what.
