# 2026-11-03

## Version 23 (rules v16): pardoned pirates and brigands may join you; a soft judge pays for it

### Mercy that recruits
After a victory over pirates or brigands you judge their leaders: execute or pardon. Now **a pardon can bring you men**. Some of the pardoned band kneel at the Sea Gate and ask to serve in the garrison:
- **Always**, if you executed the two bands before. Your mercy is rare, so they believe it.
- **Often** (one time in two) if the town calls you stern but just.
- **Seldom** (about one time in seven) if the town calls you soft: your pardon means little.

They are **20–30% of the band**, at least five men, and bring their own weapons. They ask a bounty of **6 denarii a man**: 120 brigands give about 25–35 men for roughly 150–200 denarii, far cheaper than recruiting. They also don't take townsmen from the town. The town is a little wary of them (loyalty −1). You can always send them away.

### A soft judge pays for it
If you pardon more than half of those you judge (after at least three judgements), the town calls you soft. That already cost loyalty and drew brigands. Now also:
- **pirates come more often**, as well as brigands;
- **Rome thinks less of you**: Roman favour −1 every season while you are soft.

The judgement pop-up now says all this, and the pardon option says that some may ask to serve you.

### Checked
In a direct test, two executions followed by a pardon of 120 brigands brought 29 men asking to serve, for 174 denarii. The bot simulation runs cleanly, and there are no script errors.
