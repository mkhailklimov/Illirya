# Illyria: AD 30 changelog

## 12 October 2026

**Try it:** double-click `play.bat` in the `Illirya` folder (continues the saved game, or starts one) or `play-new.bat` (new governorship). From a terminal: `python rules/play.py` / `--new`.

### Done
- **Advisors as characters.** Each of the five advisors now has a **mood** (0–5, start 3) and **one ability**, shown in the council every season: `[***..]  Knows a man: ready`.
  - **Mood:** following an advisor's advice (buy grain, recruit, hold a festival, send the convoy the way the wind favours…) raises his mood by 1; ignoring what he asks for two seasons in a row lowers it by 1. At mood 1 or below he **sulks** and will not use his ability.
  - **Abilities**, each offered at the moment it can help (you answer y/n):

    | Advisor | Ability | How often |
    |---|---|---|
    | Felix | **A season's grace:** when the chest is short at tribute time, the tribute moves to next season with no favour lost and no debt | once per governorship |
    | Nikandros | **Knows a man:** one convoy trades at +20% | once a year |
    | Marcus Antistius | **Leads the charge:** +2 after a failed battle roll that +2 would turn (not a natural 1) | once a year |
    | Epicadus | **Winter run:** one convoy may sail in winter | once per governorship |
    | Tertius Volusius | **Reads the entrails:** sees a dark omen before it is turned over and may sacrifice again for another | once a year |

  - Rules in [rules/formulas.md](../rules/formulas.md) ("The council"); ability names and texts in [rules/advisors.csv](../rules/advisors.csv). Old saves get a fresh council at mood 3.
  - Checked with 17 scripted tests (every ability, sulking, natural 1, winter refusal after the run is spent) and 8 automatic games of 13–24 seasons without errors. In those games advisors were pleased about as often as they felt ignored (51 / 54), and someone sulked about once per game: you cannot keep all five happy, which is the point.
- **Character cards** for the five advisors, 1125×1575 PNG in [design/pieces/advisors/](../design/pieces/advisors/), drawn by [design/source/advisors.py](../design/source/advisors.py) from `rules/advisors.csv`. Each card: role, a coin-medallion portrait with a Latin legend, an emblem badge, name, what he watches, five mood leaves and the ability panel. Portraits are the Tiberius coin bust with each man's mark: Epicadus a sailor's pilos and beard, Antistius a Gallic helmet with a crimson crest, Nikandros Greek curls, headband and beard, Felix a freedman's cropped hair, Tertius his head veiled by his toga for sacrifice. Emblems: galley, legion standard, amphora, coins, the augur's lituus.
- **Five draft Omen cards** (the deck had only 3), for you to choose from:
  - **e022 A comet over the bay** (once per game): forbid the talk, let it run (d20: an informer may write to Rome) or proclaim it the Emperor's glory.
  - **e023 A prophetess in the market:** let her speak, drive her out, or have Tertius test her (d20).
  - **e024 A serpent in the temple:** loyalty +3; the Illyrians' sacred snake (Cadmus and Harmonia).
  - **e025 The governor's dream** of a ship lost: sacrifice to Neptune, or risk it (d20, a ship may sink).
  - **e026 Seabirds fly inland** (only with the south wind): call the fishermen home, or risk the storm (d20).
  - Each has a historical note and source (Suetonius, Pliny, Tacitus, Ovid, Virgil) marked "check before publishing".
- **play.bat / play-new.bat:** double-click launchers for the playtest on Windows.
