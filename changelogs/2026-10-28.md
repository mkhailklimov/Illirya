# 2026-10-28

## Advisor pictures: prompts version 3

Your ChatGPT drafts of the priest and the general (now kept in `design/pieces/figures/drafts-too-detailed/`) were again a modern illustration about Rome rather than an ancient painting:
- a dark background with a glow;
- engraved hatching everywhere;
- a realistic face turned three-quarters;
- muscles and ornament on every edge.

The prompts are rewritten in [design/prompts/advisor-figures.md](../design/prompts/advisor-figures.md):
- **The target is now an Etruscan tomb painting from Tarquinia** (the Tomb of the Leopards, already in `references/`): cream plaster, skin in one flat red-brown, a wobbly brush outline, a few inner lines, a strict profile with a big almond eye, stiff naive bodies.
- **Each prompt opens with "a photograph of an ancient wall painting... not a modern illustration".** The tool then copies an object, not a mood.
- **One shared palette with hex codes.** Each advisor has one clothing colour: priest dark red, general ochre, fleet blue, economist green, treasurer white with a red border.
- **The detail-inviting words are gone:** no fringes, breastplate ornament, helmet crests, painted eyes or keys. Objects are described as simple shapes ("a liver, a rounded brown blob").
- **Five complete prompts, one per advisor**, each in its own block, style included, ready to paste.
- **A ChatGPT routine:**
  1. Start a new chat for each figure.
  2. Attach the tomb photo and have it describe the style in five points.
  3. Paste the prompt.
  4. Correct the result step by step with ready fix lines ("remove all hatching", "half as many lines").
  5. Once one figure is right, use it as the reference for the other four.
- **The vase look stays as an alternative** (two replacement paragraphs).

The game is unchanged; still version 19 (rules v14).

## Later: back to the vase (prompts version 4)

You prefer the two red-figure vases: the Kleophrades Painter's hoplite and the Berlin Painter's robed man with a staff ("judge"). The prompts are rewritten around them:
- **Two reference crops**, showing the figure only, without the pot, the glare or the room:
  - `references/crop-vase-judge.png` for the priest, the economist and the treasurer;
  - `references/crop-vase-hoplite.png` for the general and the fleet.

  Each prompt names its crop.
- **Strictly two colours**, as on the vases: flat clay orange (#D2742C) on flat black (#0E0B0A). No third colour and no glow. The advisors differ by what they hold and wear.
- **Each prompt opens with** "a single figure from an ancient Greek red-figure vase painting… in exactly the style of the attached reference… not a modern illustration, no vase shape, no reflections".
- **Profile facing left, as on both references.** Calm, upright poses; objects as plain orange shapes; no shield emblem, no helmet crest.
- **The ChatGPT routine:**
  1. Start a new chat for each figure.
  2. Attach the crop and have ChatGPT describe its style in five points.
  3. Paste the prompt.
  4. Correct the result with the ready fix lines, one at a time.
  5. Use the first good figure as a second reference for the rest.
- The fresco is kept at the end of the file as the alternative.
