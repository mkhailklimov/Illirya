# Illyria: AD 30 changelog

## 11 October 2026

**Try it:** in the `Illirya` folder run `python rules/play.py` (once: `pip install pyyaml`). `--new` starts a fresh governorship.

### Done
- **Core game concept chosen: "The Governor".** The player is a Roman governor of Acruvium (Kotor) in AD 30, guided by an AI game master. Written up in [docs/governor-concept.md](../docs/governor-concept.md) and set as Priority 1 in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
- **Design direction confirmed:** keep the mosaic and fresco style, and move the lettering to an Abecedarium-like font (Caesar Dressing recommended, Marcellus as alternative).
- **Development plan to 2030** with five phases, a demo at the end of each and a sized backlog: [docs/development-plan.md](../docs/development-plan.md).
- **Epidaurum confirmed** as the Dubrovnik-coast city for realism; Ragusa/Dubrovnik stays for a later-era expansion.
- **Task 0.1 done: lettering picked.** Caesar Dressing for the logo and big titles, Marcellus for names, dates and small labels (comparison sheet: `design/images/illyria30-font-comparison-1600x1576.png`). Fonts and their OFL licences added to `design/source/fonts`; style guide updated; all image scripts switched over and every image re-rendered (profile pictures, Facebook cover, Tiberius, Jesus, map, compass).
- **Design kit runs on Windows:** font and page URLs are built as proper `file:///` URLs, and the scripts write UTF-8.
- **Rules and event-card scaffolding** (prepares tasks 0.4–0.7): `rules/` with draft numbers, 10 draft decrees, season table, draft formulas with a worked first turn, and a 12-turn playtest log; `events/` with a card format, template and two example cards (drought, pirates off Risinium). All values are marked DRAFT for tuning by hand. A one-year check with no decrees gives treasury about +2,700 but grain falling from 6 to 1.6 months: the intended squeeze.
- **The governor's council:** five advisors (master of the fleet, general, adviser on trade, keeper of the treasury, haruspex) with draft names in `rules/advisors.csv`, a council section in the concept doc, and one event card each (e003–e007).
- **Playable in the terminal:** `python rules/play.py` plays the game from the rules files: council advice each season, up to 3 decrees, convoys, buildings, event cards with choices, save and continue, and a CSV log of every turn. Turn 1 matches the worked example in `formulas.md`.
- **Wind compass, sea current and dice:** six winds with Latin names (`rules/winds.csv`) change the harvest and convoy prices; the Adriatic's anticlockwise current favours voyages north along our coast; a dead calm stops winnowing and delays convoys. Event options can roll a d4–d20 with a bonus against a target (natural 20 always wins, 1 always fails). The drought card now comes with the south wind, the pirates card has a battle roll, and a new hill-raid battle card (e008) recalls the AD 6–9 revolt.
- **Three cards a season from three decks: Land, Sea, Omen.** All three are turned over at once before the decrees; choices can be answered in any order. Letters from Rome travel in the Sea deck. Twelve new cards (flood, earthquake, unrest, bumper harvest, coin hoard, great vintage, cheap Istrian oil, Rome demands troops, a letter from Capri, the new screw press, eagles, lightning) bring the deck to the Phase 0 target of 20, plus a quiet card per deck. The Omen deck is the thinnest (3 cards) and needs more.
- **Wind compass and d20 artwork** in the mosaic style (`design/source/winds.py`, `dice.py`): six compass images, one per wind, with the active wind glowing gold and its Latin name (AQVILO, AVSTER, FAVONIVS, AFRICVS, EVRVS, TRANQVILLITAS for calm), and the d20 with all 20 faces on a transparent background (20 glows, 1 is crimson). In `design/pieces/winds/` and `design/pieces/dice/`, ready for channel posts and the bot. `render.js` can now render with a transparent background.
- **The city and its fields:** 12 plots inside the walls (harbour and walls included) and 12 in the fields, shown as a town map each season. Twelve building types in `rules/buildings.csv` (houses cap the population; workshops, market, temple, barracks, baths; wheat terraces, vineyards & olive groves, fisheries, sheep pastures; a 2nd harbour and towers). One **Build** decree for all of them; the scribe understands "build a temple and two houses". The starting town keeps the tuned balance: Turn 1 still matches the worked example.
- **Ships** are the 9th number: 2 at the start, a convoy needs a free ship, 3 berths per harbour, a shipyard decree builds more. New Sea card: a storm can sink a ship (with a d20 rescue roll); Epicadus's galley now adds a ship. Walls and barracks now help in battle rolls.
- **Task 0.3 done: Facebook page live** at https://www.facebook.com/Illyria30/ (mirror of t.me/Illyria30 for the Community Governor posts).
- **The dialogue box:** each season the scribe states the most pressing matter and offers **A) a bold** and **B) a prudent** suggestion (worked out from the real numbers, with cost and risks shown), **C) your own words**, or D) the decree list. Free text is read by `rules/scribe.py`: Claude Opus 5.5 with structured output when an API key is set, otherwise an offline word matcher. Every decree is checked against the rules and confirmed before it is sealed; "give me 10,000 denarii" is politely refused. Convoys can take direction, cargo and amount from the words.
