# Illyria: AD 30 changelog

## 11 October 2026

### Done
- **Core game concept chosen: "The Governor".** The player is a Roman governor of Acruvium (Kotor) in AD 30, guided by an AI game master. Written up in [docs/governor-concept.md](../docs/governor-concept.md) and set as Priority 1 in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
- **Design direction confirmed:** keep the mosaic and fresco style, and move the lettering to an Abecedarium-like font (Caesar Dressing recommended, Marcellus as alternative).
- **Development plan to 2030** with five phases, a demo at the end of each and a sized backlog: [docs/development-plan.md](../docs/development-plan.md).
- **Epidaurum confirmed** as the Dubrovnik-coast city for realism; Ragusa/Dubrovnik stays for a later-era expansion.
- **Task 0.1 done: lettering picked.** Caesar Dressing for the logo and big titles, Marcellus for names, dates and small labels (comparison sheet: `design/images/illyria30-font-comparison-1600x1576.png`). Fonts and their OFL licences added to `design/source/fonts`; style guide updated; all image scripts switched over and every image re-rendered (profile pictures, Facebook cover, Tiberius, Jesus, map, compass).
- **Design kit runs on Windows:** font and page URLs are built as proper `file:///` URLs, and the scripts write UTF-8.
- **Rules and event-card scaffolding** (prepares tasks 0.4–0.7): `rules/` with draft numbers, 10 draft decrees, season table, draft formulas with a worked first turn, and a 12-turn playtest log; `events/` with a card format, template and two example cards (drought, pirates off Risinium). All values are marked DRAFT for tuning by hand. A one-year check with no decrees gives treasury about +2,700 but grain falling from 6 to 1.6 months: the intended squeeze.
