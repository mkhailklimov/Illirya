# 2026-10-29

## A shorter season report, and icons on the numbers (version 21)

### Less text after each season
The scribe's report now shows only:
- **What went well:** the three best things of the season, most important first. A victory comes before a finished building, then a profitable voyage or the tribute sent, then a triumph on the dice, then a big rise in a number.
- **What went badly:** the two worst, most important first. A disaster, a revolt or fallen men come before hunger or unpaid wages, then a storm, pirates or a loss, then a big fall in a number.
- then the season's numbers.

**The scribe's full record** (every line of the season, with the history notes) is still there, folded away: click it to open.

### Icons on the numbers
Every number in the top bar and on the season's cards now has its own small gold icon:
- grain: an ear of wheat
- treasury: a stack of coins
- people: three figures
- loyalty: a laurel wreath
- Roman favour: the eagle
- army: a helmet
- ships: a galley
- tribute to Rome: coins with an arrow down

The stores use the icon of the building that makes them (vines for wine, an olive tree for oil, sheep for fleece, the loom for cloth, the axe for timber, the salt pans, the fish, the mine for iron, the temple for marble). Any other good is an amphora.

The game rules are unchanged (rules v14). Checked in the browser over three seasons, with no script errors.
