## Version 29 (rules v18): warm black, advice that fits the card, and the aqueduct

- **Colours (A):** the page, panels, pop-ups, compass and die are now warm black instead of navy and purple. Gold lettering and the deck colours (gold Land, aquamarine Sea, magenta Omen) stay. The emblem badges on the advisor portraits are black too.
- **Advice fits the card:** on cards with no question of their own, the advisors now answer what happened. A drought brings an aqueduct or grain from the ships, not hanging brigands; an earthquake brings houses and walls; a great vintage brings a festival or a wine convoy; and so on for the eight such cards.
- **Hunger tempts the general:** when the granary is under 4.5 months, Antistius offers to march on a free tribe for its land. It is still the trap: with no just cause, the hordes come a year later.
- **New building, the aqueduct:** brings the mountain spring into town on arches (1,500 denarii, 10 timber, 4 iron, a year; no plot). When the springs run dry you lose half the grain and no loyalty. Build it from the walls on the city map; it shows as arches along the spring.
- **Checked:** every card's two proposals listed in a test, a drought played in the browser with the aqueduct built (half the loss, new advice) with no script errors, and the bot simulation runs. The random bot starves just as often as before this change.

## Version 31 (rules v19): your playtest notes, NOT yet published or tested in the browser

Your notes: the black is too sad; after each battle die it is unclear what happened; what do triumph, success, setback and disaster mean?

- **Colours:** back to the navy and purple from before version 29 (the warm black is gone; the aqueduct and everything else from v29 stays).
- **Battle, every die in numbers:** each die now says what it did, with the dead on both sides: "on target. 14 brigands fall", "they break into our line. 23 of ours fall", "we throw them back. 40 brigands fall". Each lane shows "ours 180 · brigands 140" after every throw, and a line under the dice gives the score after the volley, after their charge and after our counter-attack. (engine: fight() records killed, fell, ourLeft, foeLeft in men for every throw.)
- **Four real outcomes on every decision (your design):** every choice now lists all four before you decide, with the faces of the die for each, and the throw names the one that happened.
  - Land (e.g. the olive press on a bumper harvest): triumph = the press is ready for this very harvest, twice the extra oil; success = ready from next season; setback = it works badly, half the extra oil for a year; disaster = it works, but the Greek charges double. Buildings: half the time / as planned / a season late / built at twice the price (no more collapse).
  - Sea (a galley to Brundisium): triumph = +50% on her sales; success = as expected; setback = a third less; disaster = robbed, the cargo and the money for the return load are lost, but the ship and crew come home (no more sinking).
  - Omen (e.g. lightning on the forum): triumph = twice the effect / loyalty +3; success = as planned; setback = half the effect, or done but the town grumbles (loyalty −2); disaster = unrest, loyalty −5, and if loyalty falls below 50 the town rises that same season.
  - Card answers with no die of their own: 1.5x the good / as written / half the good / only the harm, 1.5x (before, a setback gave the same as a success).
  - A risky plan stays all or nothing: success counts as triumph, setback as disaster.
- **Checked:** syntax, and the bot simulation (5 bots x 40 games) runs with no errors.
