### 1. The battlefield (third tab)
- **Once a year a conflict.** At the first season of each year the rules decide when it comes (spring, summer or autumn) and from where: **pirates** (more likely when the town is rich or has many ships), **brigands** from inside (more likely when loyalty is low or the garrison weak), or **warriors of the tribes** (much more likely while a tribe is hostile). A **march you order** against a tribe is fought here too. The Battlefield tab shows the year's rumours ("strange sails beyond the gulf").
- **To arms:** you choose to **send Antistius** (land) or **Epicadus** (pirates), who set the order of battle and report after the fight (a lost battle sends him back wounded, mood −2), or to **lead them yourself**: +3, glory on a victory (Roman favour +3, loyalty +2 more), but **if you lose, you fall or are taken prisoner and the game ends**.
- **Your order of battle:** your garrison in cohorts of about 50, each to the left, centre or right as a **shield line** (worth about two of theirs in the clash) or as **slingers** (thin the crowd in front of them by 15% each, weaker in the clash), one cohort in **reserve** (it saves the first lost lane with a second roll). Scouts show how the enemy crowd is spread across the three lanes; every lane shows its odds with each bonus by name (the numbers, the discipline of Rome +2, their wild first charge, the walls, the barracks, the governor at their head) and the chance to win the day, live as you move cohorts.
- **The battle:** three **bloodstone** dice (new: deep green with red spots, the soldier's stone), one per lane, DC 10; two lanes of three win the day. Victory brings plunder (pirates, brigands), favour and loyalty; defeat at home costs men, a fifth of the chest and a burnt field.
- **At home the walls hold an empty flank** with the townsmen on them, so even a small garrison has a chance.
- The enemy grows 6% a year (word of a rich town spreads). With the default order of battle: pirates ~90–97%, brigands ~99%, tribes ~80% at the start, ~62% by AD 42 (leading yourself: +10 to 15 points).

### 2. Acruvium drawn as Kotor
- The city sheet is now Kotor from above: **the bay to the west** with the Sea Gate, the quay and the mole, moored ships and a shipyard on the southern spit; **the walled triangle** of twelve plots between **the river** (north) and **the spring** (south); **the mountain** to the east with the walls zigzagging up to **the crag**. The tribes' land is beyond the mountains, in the band above.
- **Each city has its character** (`rules/cities.csv`). Acruvium's: **a mountain stronghold**. The new **Citadel on the hill** (2,500 denarii, 30 timber, 10 iron, three years) gives +5 to every battle at home; 60 men against the tribes win about 60% behind the walls alone and about 91% with the citadel. **And a narrow shore:** only 8 field plots (river shore and below the spring), 4 more terraces to clear on the mountain, and the rest must be won from the tribes.
- Walls now help only at home (+2 to battles in town, not on marches); the cards' home fights (pirates, raiders, Scribonianus) count the walls and the citadel.
