### Done: balance pass on rules v2
Bots played 60 games of 40 seasons before and after the change. That confirmed the problems from 13 October: money piles up, there is too little iron and marble, the field plots are full within the first year (8 of the 12 are used at the start), and famine ended nearly half of the games.

- **New order: buy from passing merchants.** Greek and Italian ships call at the quay in the sailing season. Once a season you can buy up to 20 loads of any good except luxuries, or grain, at half again the base price (grain 750 a month). The goods are in the stores at once: no ship of ours, no voyage roll. Spare money now buys iron, marble or grain, and there is a way out of a grain crisis even when every ship is at sea. In the order desk under "Sea and trade".
- **New order: clear new fields** (600 denarii, 2 iron): one more field plot after two seasons. The four new plots are on the hillside above the bay, where the scrub is drawn; click the scrub to order the clearing. 12 field plots at the start, at most 16.
- **Iron mine:** 3 iron a season instead of 2, and you can dig two.
- The council knows the new orders. The economist asks for new fields when every field is taken, and for the merchants when grain is below 3. The general's and the haruspex's plans buy iron and marble from them. The scribe understands "buy 5 iron from the merchants" and "clear new fields".
- Small fix: "1 month of grain", not "1 months".
- Results, before → after: famine 47% → 32% of bot games; iron in year 10 about 9 → 27; marble 5 → 12–18; money in year 10 about 58,000 → 45,000. Details in [formulas.md](../rules/formulas.md), "Balance pass".
